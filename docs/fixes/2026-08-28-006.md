# 2026-08-28-006 — capture stuck confirming the wrong field, looping "could you say that again" until abandoned

*Investigated and filed as a known issue on 2026-08-25; root-caused and fixed on 2026-08-28.*
**Root cause and fix: `docs/fixes/2026-08-28-005.md`** — `last_asked_field` was re-derived by
scanning `caller_profile` for the first `pending_confirm` field instead of being stated by the
reply that actually asked a question. The original investigation below is kept as written; the
resolution is at the bottom.

## Symptom
Live call: caller said "Manos." meaning their **name**; fast-path had already moved on and asked
for **email** next. From there the call never recovered — it looped a "could you say that again"/
"let me make sure I've got that right" cycle across 6+ turns, spelling out an email address
repeatedly with zero progress, and eventually the caller gave up (`call_abandoned`), never reaching
a 3-strikes escalation despite the repeated failures. Full trace (call captured 2026-08-25, see
conversation log) shows the field/utterance mismatch below.

## Root cause (as diagnosed on 2026-08-25 — partial)
1. Caller's "Manos." (meant as the delayed answer to the *name* question) arrived after
   `node_capture_fast` had already advanced to asking about *email* — `capture_fast_gate_fallback`
   treated it as the email answer instead, extracting `email = "Manos"` at confidence 0.3.
2. That low-confidence, garbage value put `email` on the "spell it out" path
   (`LOW_CONFIDENCE_CONFIRM_THRESHOLD`, see `docs/DECISIONS.md`) — reasonable so far, since 0.3 is
   correctly rejected.
3. But every subsequent `confirm_field_answer` call in the trace is invoked with **`field="name"`**,
   not `email` — even though the caller's utterance at that point ("M-A-N-O-S at gmail dot com",
   later "M-A-N-O-S at G-M-A-I-L dot C-O-M") is unambiguously spelling out an *email*. The
   background name-verification from step 1 (confidence 0.7, below the ≥0.75 name auto-confirm bar)
   left `name` sitting in `pending_confirm`, and something in the `capture_fast_pending_confirm_
   fallback` → `node_capture` handoff keeps re-selecting `name` as the field to confirm against an
   utterance that was never an answer about the caller's name at all — so `confirm_field_answer`
   correctly returns `confirmed: false` every time (it's genuinely not a valid name), and the loop
   never terminates because the *field selection*, not the confirmation logic, is wrong.

Not yet traced to the exact line — `capture_fast_pending_confirm_fallback`'s field-selection logic
(`backend/supervisor/graph.py`, Phase 7's fast-path/Phase 3 slow-path handoff) is the prime suspect,
since `name` and `email` were both simultaneously in a not-yet-resolved state (`name` from the
background verifier at confidence 0.7, `email` from the garbage fast-path extraction) and the
fallback picked the wrong one to keep re-asking about.

## What was ruled out
- Not the same bug as `docs/fixes/2026-08-24-005.md` (background verification's urgent-reask could
  never fire) or `docs/fixes/2026-08-24-009.md` (an unrelated earlier pending field silently
  auto-confirmed) — both of those are about a field being wrongly *resolved*; this is a field being
  wrongly *selected* for repeated re-confirmation while never advancing.
- Not a `confirm_field_answer`/Claude reliability issue by itself — its individual judgments
  (`confirmed: false`, `needs_clarification: true`) are locally correct for the (wrong) field it
  was asked to check against.
- Separately, the 3-consecutive-Claude-failure system_error escalation path (CLAUDE.md rule #7)
  also didn't trigger even though `confirm_field_answer` hit `llm_call_failed` once (trace #100) —
  worth checking whether that counter is scoped per-field/per-turn in a way that let it reset
  before reaching 3, though the call had far more than 3 total failed *turns* by the time it was
  abandoned.

## Hypotheses at the time (superseded — see Resolution)
1. `capture_fast_pending_confirm_fallback`'s field-selection should prefer whichever field the
   caller's *current* utterance plausibly answers (e.g. contains "@"/spelled-out "at"/"dot" →
   email) over whatever field happens to be sitin `pending_confirm` from an unrelated earlier turn.
2. ~~The 3-strikes escalation counter may need to trigger on repeated `confirmed: false` for the same
   field/turn shape, not just on raw Claude call failures~~ — **resolved, see
   `docs/fixes/2026-08-28-004.md`.** The escalation never fired here because
   `_delayed_failure_reask` never incremented `attempts` at all: the bookkeeping was supposed to
   happen in an urgent-reask fallback that had since been removed. Background verification failures
   now count toward the 3 strikes and escalate on the third. This does NOT resolve this entry's own
   subject — the wrong *field* being selected for repeated re-confirmation — but it does mean that
   loop is now bounded and ends in a handoff to a human rather than running until the caller hangs
   up.
3. Root-cause the exact fallback branch before deciding a fix — not yet confirmed which specific
   condition in `node_capture_fast`/`_fallback_to_real_capture` picks `name` here instead of `email`.

No fix applied yet — flagging for the user to decide whether to prioritize this over other work
before touching `graph.py`'s capture-fast-path fallback logic.

---

## Resolution (2026-08-28)

Hypothesis 3 was the right instinct — root-cause the exact branch first. The branch was
`_sync_last_asked_field`, called from `_fallback_to_real_capture`. After step 1's low-confidence
email was rejected and re-asked, it scanned the profile, found `name` sitting in `pending_confirm`
at 0.7 from the background verifier, and returned `name` as the field the caller's next utterance
would answer — even though the question just spoken was about the email. Every following turn then
entered `capture_fast_pending_confirm_fallback` with `field="name"` and ran `confirm_field_answer`
against `name` while the caller spelled out an email address, which is exactly what step 3
observed and could not explain. `needs_clarification` returns without touching `caller_profile`,
so the wrong value was never dislodged and the loop could not terminate.

Hypothesis 1 (prefer whichever field the utterance plausibly answers, by looking for "@"/"at"/
"dot") was aimed at the right place but would have layered a second guess on top of the first. The
field a reply is about is known for certain at the moment the reply is built, so `node_capture`
now states it and nothing re-derives it. See `docs/fixes/2026-08-28-005.md`.

Hypothesis 2 (the escalation never firing) turned out to be an independent defect with its own
cause — `_delayed_failure_reask` never incremented `attempts`, because the bookkeeping lived in an
urgent-reask fallback that had since been deleted. Fixed separately in
`docs/fixes/2026-08-28-004.md`.

The "what was ruled out" section holds up: this was neither `2026-08-24-005` nor `2026-08-24-009`,
and `confirm_field_answer` was indeed behaving correctly for the (wrong) field it was handed.
`2026-08-24-009` is in fact the mirror image of this bug — it guarded which field an utterance may
be treated as confirming (inbound), while this one corrupted which field was recorded as
outstanding (outbound).
