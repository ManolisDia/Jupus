# 2026-08-28-007 — "I just want to speak to a real human" didn't escalate, and the booking re-ask it landed in had no ceiling

## Symptom
Live call. Capture and research went cleanly through to booking, then:

```
caller: "Actually, stop. I don't want to do this. I actually just want to
         speak to a real human."
agent:  "Sorry, I didn't catch a date or time there — what day and time
         would work for you?"
caller: "I just want to speak to a real human."
agent:  "Sorry, I didn't catch a date or time there — what day and time
         would work for you?"
                                                        -> call_abandoned
```

Trace #114–#139. The caller asked for a human twice, in plain language, and was asked for a
booking time both times.

## Root cause
Two defects, one behind the other.

**1. `heuristics.is_explicit_human_request` missed the phrasing.** It was a flat list of twelve
literal substrings, checked on every turn before the graph runs. It carried `"talk to a human"`
and `"real person"` — but not `"speak to a human"`, and not `"real human"`. So
`"I just want to speak to a real human"` matched nothing at all.

The gap isn't any one missing phrase. A flat list has to enumerate a cross-product it can never
finish: {speak, talk, chat} × {to, with} × {a, a real, an actual, a live, …} × {human, person,
someone, somebody, agent, operator, …}. The twelve entries covered an arbitrary scattering of that
space, and the caller happened to land outside it. Nothing about "speak to a real human" is
unusual — it is arguably the single most natural way to say it.

**2. The branch it fell through to loops forever.** `node_booking`'s `_handle_time_request`
answers an unparseable date with "Sorry, I didn't catch a date or time there" and no attempt
counter. Every other "I didn't understand you" path in the graph is bounded —
`retry_counts["classification"]` at 2 → `unable_to_classify`, `retry_counts["research_gather"]` at
2 → give up gracefully, capture's `attempts` at 3 → `capture_failed`. This one was the exception,
so a caller who wants anything other than a booking gets the same sentence until they hang up. It
is the same failure mode as `docs/fixes/2026-08-28-004.md`, in a different node: not a wrong
answer, an unbounded one.

Defect 1 is what the caller hit; defect 2 is why the call was unrecoverable rather than merely
annoying. Either alone would have been survivable.

## Fix
**The escalation check now matches structure, not phrasing.** The twelve literals are kept exactly
as they were and checked first — they're proven, and several are bare nouns (`"representative"`)
that a structural pattern shouldn't reach — with regexes layered on top for the productive forms:
`speak|talk|chat` + `to|with` + a bounded qualifier gap + a human noun; `get|give` + article +
human noun; `transfer me` / `connect me` / `put me through`; and a bare qualifier + noun
(`"a real human"`). Union semantics, so the rewrite can only ever widen — every legacy phrase is
asserted still to match.

Two exclusions are deliberate and load-bearing:
- **No lawyer/solicitor/attorney in the noun set.** "I want to speak to a lawyer" is what every
  caller on this line wants, and it is exactly what booking a consultation delivers. Matching it
  would hand every successful call to a human.
- **`put me through`, never bare `put me`.** "Put me down for Tuesday at ten" is a booking, not an
  exit.

Also excluded by construction: past tense (`"I spoke to someone at the council last week"` is a
caller describing their situation) and the article-less `get|give` form, so `"I need someone to
help me with my landlord"` — a description of need — doesn't fire. Still plain regex, no LLM on
this path, per CLAUDE.md rule 2.

**The booking re-ask is bounded** by `retry_counts["booking_datetime"]`, escalating on the third
consecutive unusable answer with a new `booking_failed` reason — matching capture's limit of 3, the
closest analogue, since both are "we cannot extract the thing we need from what they keep saying."
Any turn whose date/time *is* understood resets the counter to 0, so two mumbles followed by a real
answer don't leave the caller one bad utterance away from an escalation later in the same booking.
`booking_failed` is the seventh escalation reason; `docs/reference/`'s three "one of six" claims
and both of its tables are updated. `eval/insights_agent.py`'s histogram is keyed generically and
needed no change.

## Verification
`pytest backend/tests eval/tests` — 461 pass (419 before, +42, most of them parametrised
heuristic cases), plus `scripts/check_architecture.py`. 13 of the new cases were confirmed to fail
against the old code, including both live utterances verbatim; the 15 legacy-phrase cases pass
both before and after, which is what makes "the rewrite only widens" a checked claim rather than
an intention. The negative set covers the lawyer case, the "put me down for" case, past tense,
"I need someone", and ordinary intake answers from real traces. Not yet re-verified on a live call.
